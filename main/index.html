              Founder's Note

              A note from the founder

              I kept worlds long before I knew what to do with them. Some arrived as names. Some arrived as voices. Some arrived as places I could not stop returning to.

              Most people collect things. I collected worlds. Eventually they needed more than somewhere to live. They needed a place to be built, studied, protected, and carried forward.

              That place became House of Worlds.

              JDONAI Founder
